# View Line of Business Details

## Overview

View detailed information about a specific Line of Business. This page displays all current information and allows you to access editing and deletion options.

## How to View Line of Business Details

### Method 1: From the List View
1. Navigate to **Configuration → Line of Business**
2. Click on any business line name in the list
3. The details page opens showing complete information

### Method 2: Using Search
1. Use the search field on the list page to find a specific business line
2. Click on the matching result
3. The details page loads

## Details Page Information

The Line of Business details page displays:

### Basic Information
- **Name** - The business line identifier (e.g., "GSM", "Internet")
- **Description** - Detailed explanation of the business line's purpose and scope
- **Created Date** - Timestamp when the business line was created
- **Last Updated** - Timestamp of the most recent modification

### Available Actions

#### Edit Business Line
- Click the **Edit** button to modify the business line name or description
- Opens the edit form in a modal
- See [Edit Line of Business](./edit-line-of-business) for detailed steps

#### Delete Business Line
- Click the **Delete** button to remove the business line
- A confirmation dialog appears asking to confirm deletion
- Once deleted, the business line cannot be recovered
- The action is permanent

#### Back to List
- Click the **Back** button or navigation link to return to the Line of Business list

## Understanding Business Line Status

Business lines in Sentra CVM are:
- **Always Active** - All business lines are active by default in the system
- **In Use** - Business lines may be referenced by campaigns, offers, or other configurations
- **Deletable** - Can only be deleted if not actively used in other features
