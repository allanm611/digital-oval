# Line of Business

## Overview

The Line of Business page displays all business lines and services configured in your Sentra CVM platform. Business lines represent different service categories or divisions within your organization (e.g., GSM, Internet, Fixed Line, Enterprise Solutions).

## Accessing the Line of Business List

1. Navigate to **Configuration** from the main menu
2. Select **Line of Business** from the configuration options
3. The system displays all configured business lines in a list view

## List Page Features

### Search
- Use the search field to find business lines by name or description
- Search is performed in real-time as you type

### Display Information

Each business line in the list shows:
- **Name** - The business line identifier
- **Description** - A brief explanation of the business line's purpose
- **Created Date** - When the business line was created
- **Last Updated** - Most recent modification date

### Actions

For each business line, you can:
- **View Details** - Click on any line to see full information
- **Edit** - Modify the business line name or description
- **Delete** - Remove the business line from the system (with confirmation)

## Default Business Lines

The system comes with pre-configured business lines:
- **GSM** - Global System for Mobile Communications (mobile network services)
- **Internet** - Broadband and internet services
- **Fixed Line** - Traditional landline telephone services
- **Enterprise Solutions** - Business telecommunications and IT services
- **Digital Services** - Digital transformation and cloud services
