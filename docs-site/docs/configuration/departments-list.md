# Departments

## Overview

Departments represent organizational units within your company that participate in campaigns and customer interactions. Each department has specific responsibilities and roles in your marketing and business operations.

## Default Departments

Your system comes with five pre-configured departments:

### 1. Marketing
- **Description:** Responsible for marketing campaigns and customer acquisition
- **Focus:** Campaign creation, audience targeting, messaging strategy
- **Activities:** Campaign planning, audience segmentation, content creation
- **Metrics:** Conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, campaign ROI

### 2. Sales
- **Description:** Handles sales operations and customer relationships
- **Focus:** Sales-driven campaigns, customer relationship management
- **Activities:** Lead nurturing, sales enablement, customer engagement
- **Metrics:** Deal closure rate, sales pipeline, revenue impact

### 3. Customer Support
- **Description:** Provides customer service and technical support
- **Focus:** Customer satisfaction, issue resolution, support communications
- **Activities:** Support campaigns, issue notifications, satisfaction surveys
- **Metrics:** Customer satisfaction score, resolution time, ticket volume

### 4. Product Management
- **Description:** Manages product development and strategy
- **Focus:** Product communications, feature announcements, user adoption
- **Activities:** Product launch campaigns, feature announcements, user education
- **Metrics:** Product adoption rate, feature usage, user feedback

### 5. Finance
- **Description:** Handles financial operations and budget management
- **Focus:** Financial communications, billing, budget oversight
- **Activities:** Invoice communications, billing notifications, payment reminders
- **Metrics:** Collection rate, budget adherence, cost efficiency

## Accessing Departments

1. Navigate to **Configuration** from the main menu
2. Select **Departments**
3. The system displays all configured departments in a list view

## Departments List Features

### Search & Filter
- Use the search field to find departments by name or description
- Real-time search results
- Quick filtering capability

### Display Information

Each department shows:
- **Name** - The department identifier
- **Description** - Detailed explanation of department role
- **Created Date** - When the department was created
- **Last Updated** - Most recent modification date

### Available Actions

For each department, you can:
- **View Details** - Click department name to see full information
- **Edit** - Modify the department name or description
- **Delete** - Remove the department from the system

## Using Departments

### In Campaigns

When creating campaigns:
1. Assign campaigns to responsible department
2. Helps track which department owns campaign
3. Enables department-specific reporting
4. Facilitates team coordination

### In Team Management

For organizational structure:
1. Assign team members to departments
2. Set department responsibilities
3. Configure department permissions
4. Manage department budgets

### In Reporting

Track metrics by department:
- Campaign performance by department
- Revenue impact by department
- Team productivity metrics
- Department contribution to goals
