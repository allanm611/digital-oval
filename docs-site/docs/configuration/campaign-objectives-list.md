# Campaign Objectives

## Overview

Campaign Objectives define the goals and purposes of your marketing campaigns. Each objective represents a business strategy or customer outcome that your campaigns aim to achieve. Objectives help organize campaigns, guide strategy, and measure success.

## Default Campaign Objectives

Your system comes with five pre-configured objectives:

### 1. New Customer Acquisition
- **Purpose:** Attract and convert new customers to your service
- **Focus:** Growth through new customer onboarding
- **Typical Metrics:** Conversion rate, customer acquisition cost
- **Campaign Type:** Awareness, Lead generation, Conversion campaigns

### 2. Customer Retention
- **Purpose:** Keep existing customers engaged and loyal
- **Focus:** Maintaining current customer base
- **Typical Metrics:** Churn rate, engagement rate, customer lifetime value
- **Campaign Type:** Loyalty, Engagement, Re-engagement campaigns

### 3. Churn Prevention
- **Purpose:** Prevent at-risk customers from leaving
- **Focus:** Identifying and saving at-risk customers
- **Typical Metrics:** Retention rate, win-back rate
- **Campaign Type:** Retention, Winback, Reactivation campaigns

### 4. Upsell/Cross-sell
- **Purpose:** Increase revenue from existing customers
- **Focus:** Growing value per customer
- **Typical Metrics:** Average order value, revenue increase
- **Campaign Type:** Promotional, Product education, Sales campaigns

### 5. Dormant Customer Reactivation
- **Purpose:** Re-engage inactive or dormant customers
- **Focus:** Bringing back inactive customers
- **Typical Metrics:** Reactivation rate, revenue recovery
- **Campaign Type:** Winback, Incentive, Engagement campaigns

## Accessing Campaign Objectives

1. Navigate to **Configuration** from the main menu
2. Select **Campaign Objectives**
3. The system displays all configured objectives in a list view

## Campaign Objectives List Features

### Search & Filter
- Use the search field to find objectives by name or description
- Real-time search results
- Quick filtering capability

### Display Information

Each objective shows:
- **Name** - The objective identifier
- **Description** - Detailed explanation of the objective
- **Created Date** - When the objective was created
- **Last Updated** - Most recent modification date

### Available Actions

For each objective, you can:
- **View Details** - Click objective name to see full information
- **Edit** - Modify the objective name or description
- **Delete** - Remove the objective from the system

## Best Practices

### Objective Selection
- Choose objectives that clearly align with campaign purpose
- Use objectives to create campaign categories
- Review objective match during campaign review
- Document objective selection rationale

### Objective Management
- Create custom objectives for specific needs
- Keep objective list focused (avoid too many)
- Review and archive unused objectives
- Consolidate overlapping objectives

### Campaign Organization
- Use objectives to segment campaigns
- Generate reports by objective
- Track performance by objective
- Measure success against objective goals

## Reporting by Objective

Track performance across objectives:

**By New Customer Acquisition Objective**
- New customers acquired
- Acquisition cost
- Conversion rate
- Source performance

**By Customer Retention Objective**
- Engagement rate
- Customer lifetime value
- Repeat purchase rate
- Churn rate

**By Churn Prevention Objective**
- At-risk customers identified
- Retention rate of targeted customers
- Cost per saved customer

**By Upsell/Cross-sell Objective**
- Revenue increase per customer
- Uplift percentage
- Average order value
- Product affinity

**By Dormant Reactivation Objective**
- Reactivation rate
- Cost per reactivation
- Revenue recovered
