# Campaign Reports

## Overview

Campaign Reports provide detailed analytics and performance metrics for your campaigns. Track delivery, engagement, conversions, and ROI to measure campaign success and make data-driven improvements.

## Key Metrics

### Delivery Metrics
- **Scheduled** - Total messages scheduled to send
- **Sent** - Messages successfully sent to customers
- **Delivered** - Messages delivered to customer devices
- **Failed** - Messages that failed to send
- **Delivery Rate** - Percentage of messages delivered

[Insert screenshot of delivery metrics]

---

### Engagement Metrics
- **Opened** - Unique customers who opened the message
- **Clicked** - Unique customers who clicked a link
- **Unsubscribed** - Customers who unsubscribed after message
- **Open Rate** - % of delivered messages that were opened
- **Click Rate** - % of delivered messages that were clicked

### Conversion Metrics
- **Conversions** - Completed desired actions
- **Conversion Rate** - % of delivered messages leading to conversion
- **Revenue** - Total revenue attributed to campaign
- **ROI** - Return on investment
- **ROAS** - Return on ad spend

## Tips for Better Performance

1. **Monitor Early** - Check reports 24 hours after launch
2. **Compare Segments** - See which segments perform best
3. **Analyze Channels** - Identify best-performing channels
4. **Track Trends** - Look for patterns over time
5. **Optimize Timing** - Send at times with highest engagement
6. **A/B Test** - Use control groups to measure impact
7. **Document Learnings** - Note insights for future campaigns

## Common Questions

### When Do Reports Update?
- Data updates hourly after campaign execution
- Real-time metrics available for active campaigns
- Final metrics available 24-48 hours after completion

### Why Are My Metrics Zero?
- Campaign may still be executing
- Messages may not have been sent yet
- Check campaign status and date range
- Refresh page to load latest data

### How Do I Compare Campaigns?
- Use the campaign comparison feature
- Export data for manual comparison
- Run similar date ranges for fair comparison
