# Campaign Catalogs

## Overview

Campaign Catalogs allow you to organize and categorize your campaigns for better management and classification. Group campaigns by type, department, region, or any other organizational structure that fits your business needs.

## What are Catalogs?

Catalogs are **organizational structures** that help you:
- Group related campaigns together
- Organize campaigns by business unit
- Classify campaigns by purpose or region
- Make campaigns easier to find and manage
