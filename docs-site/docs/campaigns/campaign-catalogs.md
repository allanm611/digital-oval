# Campaign Catalogs

## Overview

Campaign Catalogs (also called Campaign Categories) allow you to organize and categorize your campaigns for better management and classification. Group campaigns by type, department, region, or any other organizational structure that fits your business needs.

---

## What are Catalogs?

Catalogs are **organizational structures** that help you:
- Group related campaigns together
- Organize campaigns by business unit, type, or region
- Classify campaigns by purpose
- Make campaigns easier to find and filter
- Track campaigns by organizational category

---

## Catalog Fields

Each campaign catalog contains:

- **ID** - Unique identifier for the catalog
- **Name** - Catalog name (required, max 100 characters)
- **Description** - Optional description of the catalog (max 500 characters)
- **Parent Category** - Can have hierarchical structure with parent catalogs
- **Display Order** - Controls the order catalogs appear in lists
- **Is Active** - Status flag to enable/disable catalogs
- **Created By** - User who created the catalog
- **Created At / Updated At** - Timestamps for creation and modification

---

## Using Catalogs

### Assigning Catalogs When Creating a Campaign

When creating or editing a campaign, you select a catalog:

1. Go to [Create Campaign](./create-campaign) → **Step 1: Definition**
2. In the "Campaign Catalog" field, select from your available catalogs
3. Choose the catalog that best organizes this campaign
4. Continue with campaign configuration

Each campaign can only belong to one catalog.

### Filtering Campaigns by Catalog

View campaigns organized by their catalog assignment to see related campaigns grouped together.

---

## Tips

- Use consistent naming for catalogs that reflects your organizational structure
- Keep catalog list manageable (use hierarchical parent/child relationships if needed)
- Document what each catalog is intended for so your team understands the organization
- Use catalogs consistently across similar campaign types
- Review and clean up unused catalogs periodically
