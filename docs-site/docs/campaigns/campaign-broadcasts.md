# Campaign Broadcasts

## Overview

Campaign Broadcasts allow you to execute and distribute your campaigns to target audiences through various delivery channels. Once a campaign is approved, you can broadcast it immediately or schedule it for a future date.

---

## What is a Broadcast?

A broadcast is the **execution and delivery** of your campaign:
- Sends messages to selected segments
- Follows configured delivery flows
- Adheres to scheduled timing
- Respects customer preferences (DND, opt-outs)

---

## Before Broadcasting

### Campaign Requirements

Before you can broadcast, ensure:
- ✅ Campaign is in **Approved** status
- ✅ Campaign has **Definition** (name, objective, dates)
- ✅ Campaign has **Audience** (segments selected)
- ✅ Campaign has **Delivery Flows** configured
- ✅ Campaign has **Scheduling** set
- ✅ All validation checks pass

### Pre-Broadcast Checklist

- [ ] Reviewed all campaign details
- [ ] Verified target audience is correct
- [ ] Confirmed delivery channels and templates
- [ ] Checked scheduling (date, time, timezone)
- [ ] Verified no business logic errors
- [ ] Approved campaign is finalized

## Pausing a Broadcast

### How to Pause

While a broadcast is executing:
1. Go to campaign details
2. Click **Pause** button
3. Broadcast stops immediately

### When to Pause

- Found an error in messaging
- Need to fix targeting
- Business decision to stop
- Technical issues detected

### Resuming After Pause

Click **Resume** to continue broadcast
- Continues with remaining messages
- Resumes schedule if applicable

## Handling Broadcast Errors

### Common Issues

**Error:** Low delivery rate
- Check network connectivity
- Verify phone numbers/emails valid
- Check carrier restrictions

**Error:** Message formatting issues
- Review message templates
- Check for missing variables
- Verify HTML/text formatting

**Error:** Timeout errors
- Check large audience size
- Verify server capacity
- Consider scheduled broadcast

### Error Resolution

1. Pause the broadcast
2. Identify the issue
3. Fix campaign settings if needed
4. Resume or re-execute broadcast

## Delivery Considerations

### Compliance & Preferences

Broadcasts respect:
- **DND (Do Not Disturb) Lists** - Excluded customers
- **Opt-Out Preferences** - Unsubscribed customers
- **Consent Rules** - Regulatory requirements
- **Channel Preferences** - Customer preferences

### Delivery Timing

- **Timezone Optimization** - Send at customer timezone
- **Quiet Hours** - Respect nighttime restrictions
- **Business Hours** - For business communications
- **Peak Times** - Times with highest engagement

## Best Practices

1. **Test First** - Always use control groups
2. **Verify Timing** - Check timezone settings
3. **Check Lists** - Verify DND and opt-out lists are current
4. **Monitor Start** - Watch first hour for errors
5. **Document Results** - Save learnings from broadcast
6. **Plan Ahead** - Schedule broadcasts in advance
7. **Have Backup Plan** - Know how to pause if needed
