# Create Campaign

## Overview

The campaign creation process is a guided workflow that walks you through all the necessary steps to set up a complete campaign. You'll configure campaign details, select your target audience, set up delivery flows, schedule execution, and preview before launch.

---

## Campaign Creation Flow

The campaign creation consists of **5 steps**:

### Step 1: Definition

**What you'll configure:**
- **Campaign Name** - A descriptive name for your campaign
- **Description** - Optional details about the campaign purpose
- **Campaign Objective** - Choose from:
  - New Customer Acquisition
  - Customer Retention
  - Churn Prevention
  - Upsell/Cross-sell
  - Dormant Customer Reactivation
- **Campaign Category** - Select the category this campaign belongs to
- **Budget** - Allocated budget (if applicable)

---

### Step 2: Audience

**What you'll configure:**
- **Select Segments** - Choose target customer segments
- **Segment-Offer Mapping** - Map which offers apply to each segment
- **Control Groups** - Optional: Set up control groups to measure campaign impact

**Control Group Setup:**
- **Enable Control Group** - Toggle to enable
- **Control Group Percentage** - % of audience to exclude (typically 5-10%)
- **Type** - Standard control group configuration

**Screenshot:**
[Insert screenshot of audience configuration step]

**Important:** Ensure your selected segments align with your campaign objective.

---

### Step 3: Delivery Flows

**What you'll configure:**
- **Delivery Channels** - Set up how messages reach customers (Email, SMS, Push, etc.)
- **Message Templates** - Select or create message templates for each channel
- **Flow Conditions** - Define when messages should be sent
- **Trigger Rules** - Set up behavioral or time-based triggers

**Screenshot:**
[Insert screenshot of delivery flows step]

**Note:** Delivery flows determine the customer experience and message cadence.

---

### Step 4: Scheduling

**What you'll configure:**
- **Start Date & Time** - When the campaign begins
- **End Date** - Campaign expiration date (optional)
- **Execution Schedule:**
  - **Immediate** - Execute as soon as approved
  - **Scheduled** - Execute at a specific date/time
  - **Recurring** - Execute on a recurring schedule
- **Timezone** - Select the timezone for execution

**Screenshot:**
[Insert screenshot of scheduling step]

**Tip:** Consider customer timezone preferences for better engagement.

---

### Step 5: Preview & Launch

**What you'll review:**
- **Campaign Summary** - All campaign details at a glance
- **Audience Count** - Total number of customers in the campaign
- **Segment-Offer Mapping** - Verification of which offers go to which segments
- **Delivery Configuration** - Review of all delivery flows
- **Schedule Details** - Confirmation of start/end times

**Actions Available:**
- **Save as Draft** - Save progress and continue later (auto-saved)
- **Submit for Approval** - Submit the campaign for approval
- **Edit** - Go back to previous steps to make changes

## Navigation

- **Next Button** - Move to the next step (validates current step)
- **Back Button** - Return to previous step
- **Step Indicators** - Click on any completed step to jump back and edit

---

## Saving Progress

Your campaign progress is automatically saved as a draft. You can:
- **Close and Return Later** - Your progress is saved automatically
- **Submit for Approval** - When ready to launch
- **Make Edits** - Return to any step to modify details before submission

---

## After Creation

Once you've created your campaign:

1. **Approval Process** - Campaign goes to approval queue
2. **Approver Review** - Assigned approvers review and approve/reject
3. **Ready to Execute** - Once approved, you can execute the campaign
4. **Monitor Performance** - Track results on the [Campaign Reports](./campaign-reports) page

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Incomplete Audience Setup** - Ensure all segments are properly selected
2. ❌ **Missing Delivery Flows** - Configure flows for all planned channels
3. ❌ **Unclear Campaign Objectives** - Choose specific, measurable objectives
4. ❌ **Scheduling Conflicts** - Check for timezone issues before scheduling
5. ❌ **Skipping Preview** - Always review all details before submitting
