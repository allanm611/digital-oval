# Customer Reports

## Overview

Customer Reports provide analytics on customer behavior, engagement, and lifecycle metrics to help you understand and optimize your customer relationships.

## Key Metrics

### Customer Base Metrics
- **Total Customers** - Total number of customers
- **Active Customers** - Customers who engaged recently
- **Inactive Customers** - Dormant accounts
- **New Customers** - Recently added customers
- **Churn Rate** - % of customers who became inactive

**Screenshot:**
[Insert screenshot of base metrics]


### Engagement Metrics
- **Email Opens** - Customers who opened emails
- **Email Clicks** - Customers who clicked links
- **SMS Responses** - SMS interaction rate
- **Campaign Participation** - Campaigns customer joined
- **Engagement Score** - Overall engagement level


### Transaction Metrics
- **Total Purchases** - Number of transactions
- **Total Revenue** - Revenue from customer
- **Average Order Value** - Mean purchase amount
- **Customer Lifetime Value** - Total expected value
- **Repeat Purchase Rate** - % of repeat customers

## Report Types

### Customer Segmentation Report
Breakdown of customers by segment:
- **Segment Name** - Name of segment
- **Customer Count** - How many in segment
- **Engagement** - Segment engagement level
- **Revenue** - Revenue from segment

### Customer Lifecycle Report
Track customer journey stages:
- **Acquisition** - New customers
- **Active** - Engaged customers
- **At Risk** - Potentially churning
- **Churned** - Inactive customers
- **Reactivated** - Returned customers
