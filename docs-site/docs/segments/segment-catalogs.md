# Segment Catalogs

## Overview

Segment Catalogs allow you to organize and categorize your segments for better management and classification. Group segments by type, purpose, region, or any other organizational structure that fits your business needs.

## What are Catalogs?

Catalogs are **organizational structures** that help you:
- Group related segments together
- Organize segments by type or purpose
- Classify segments by business unit or region
- Make segments easier to find and manage
