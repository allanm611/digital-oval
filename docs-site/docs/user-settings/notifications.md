# Notifications

## Overview

Notification settings allow you to control when and how you receive updates about important platform events, including campaign status changes, offer approvals, segment updates, and system alerts.


## Notification Preferences

You can customize your notification settings to control:

### Notification Types

**Campaign Notifications**
- Campaign creation and approval status changes
- Campaign launch and completion updates
- Campaign performance alerts
- Campaign scheduling notifications

**Offer Notifications**
- Offer approval requests
- Offer status changes (active, paused, archived)
- Offer expiration alerts
- Offer performance updates

**Segment Notifications**
- Segment creation confirmations
- Segment refresh status
- Segment data quality alerts
- Segment membership updates

**System Notifications**
- Failed jobs or scheduled tasks
- Infrastructure alerts
- User permission changes
- Configuration updates

### Notification Channels

Receive notifications through:
- **In-Platform** - Notifications displayed in the platform bell icon
- **Email** - Email notifications sent to your registered email
- **System Alerts** - Critical system-level alerts


## Accessing Notification Settings

1. Click your **profile icon** in the top-right corner
2. Select **Settings**
3. Navigate to the **Notifications** section
4. Adjust your preferences and save


## Managing Notifications

### Enable/Disable Notification Types

Toggle each notification type on or off based on your preferences:
- You can selectively disable certain notification types
- Critical alerts cannot be disabled for security and compliance reasons

### Select Notification Channels

Choose how you want to receive notifications:
- Select your preferred channels (email, in-platform, etc.)
- You can enable multiple channels simultaneously
- Email notifications will be sent to your registered email address

### Notification Frequency

Control how often you receive notifications:
- **Real-time** - Immediate notifications as events occur
- **Daily Digest** - Single daily summary of all notifications
- **Weekly Digest** - Weekly summary of notifications


## In-Platform Notifications

The notification bell icon in the top navigation shows:
- Number of unread notifications (badge count)
- Recent notifications when clicked
- Link to mark notifications as read
- Archive notifications you no longer need


## Email Notifications

Email notifications include:
- Item name and relevant details
- Direct link to view the item or take action
- Your notification preference summary

To update your email address:
1. Go to **My Profile** in your user settings
2. Update your email address
3. Verify the new email address if required


## Do Not Disturb

Set Do Not Disturb (DND) hours to pause notifications during specific times:
- **Start Time** - When DND begins
- **End Time** - When DND ends
- **Days** - Which days DND is active (weekdays, weekends, all days)

During DND hours:
- Notifications are queued and delivered after the period ends
- Critical alerts may still be sent if essential
- You can manually override DND if needed


## Notification History

View your notification history:
1. Click the **notification bell** icon in the top navigation
2. View recent notifications (typically last 30 days)
3. Search notifications by item name or type
4. Archive old notifications to clean up the list


## Tips

- Regular users should enable campaign and offer notifications
- Administrators should enable all notifications to stay aware of system changes
- Use email digests if you receive too many in-platform notifications
- Set DND hours to avoid notifications outside working hours
- Check notification settings regularly to ensure they match your current role
