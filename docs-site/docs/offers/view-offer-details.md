# View Offer Details

## Overview

The Offer Details page shows all information about a specific offer and available management actions.

## Offer Information

- **Name & Description** - Offer details
- **Type** - Category of offer
- **Discount/Value** - Offer details
- **Quantity** - Available quantity
- **Expiration Date** - When offer ends
- **Status** - Current offer status

---

## Action Buttons

### Edit Offer
Modify offer details and configuration.

### Pause Offer
Temporarily disable the offer.

### Resume Offer
Re-enable a paused offer.

### Archive Offer
Move to archived state.

### Delete Offer
Permanently remove the offer.
