# Offer Catalogs

## Overview

Offer Catalogs allow you to organize and categorize your offers for better management and classification. Group offers by type, category, promotion period, or any other organizational structure that fits your business needs.

## What are Catalogs?

Catalogs are **organizational structures** that help you:
- Group related offers together
- Organize offers by type or promotion
- Classify offers by department or region
- Make offers easier to find and manage
