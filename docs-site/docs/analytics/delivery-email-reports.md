# Delivery Email Reports

## Overview

Email Delivery Reports track the health and performance of your email channel. Monitor delivery rates, opens, clicks, and bounces to optimize email campaigns and improve deliverability.

## Key Metrics

### Hero Metrics

**Email Performance Overview:**

**Delivery Statistics**
- **Total Emails:** Sent in period
- **Successfully Delivered:** Inbox placement
- **Bounced:** Hard/soft bounce
- **Delivery Rate:** % delivered

**Engagement**
- **Opened:** Recipients opening email
- **Open Rate:** % of delivered opened
- **Clicked:** Recipients clicking links
- **Click Rate:** % engagement

**Health**
- **Bounce Rate:** % bouncing

- **Complaint Rate:** % as spam
- **Spam Score:** Deliverability risk

## KPIs & Metrics Explained

**Delivery Rate** - % emails reaching inbox
- Formula: Delivered / Sent × 100
- Target: &gt;95%
- Benchmark: 98-99%

**Open Rate** - % opening email
- Formula: Opens / Delivered × 100
- Target: 20-30%
- Varies by segment/type

**Click Rate** - % clicking links
- Formula: Clicks / Delivered × 100
- Target: 2-5%
- Indicates engagement

**Bounce Rate** - % bouncing
- Formula: Bounced / Sent × 100
- Target: &lt;3%
- Monitor closely

## Troubleshooting

### Low Delivery Rate
- **Cause:** Spam filters, authentication issues
- **Solution:** Review content, add authentication
- **Prevention:** Monitor and maintain reputation

### Low Open Rate
- **Cause:** Subject line, send time, list quality
- **Solution:** A/B test, optimize send time
- **Prevention:** Segment and personalize

### High Bounce Rate
- **Cause:** Invalid emails, hard bounces
- **Solution:** Validate before send, clean list
- **Prevention:** Validation at import

### Spam Complaints
- **Cause:** Unwanted email, list quality
- **Solution:** Honor opt-outs, clean list
- **Prevention:** Maintain list quality
