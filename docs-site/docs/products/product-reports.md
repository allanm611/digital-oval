# Product Reports

## Overview

Product Reports provide analytics on product performance, sales, and customer engagement.

---

## Key Metrics

### Sales Metrics
- **Units Sold** - Total units sold
- **Revenue** - Total revenue from product
- **Average Price** - Average selling price
- **Top Performing Product** - Best seller

### Engagement Metrics
- **Customer Views** - Times viewed by customers
- **Click Rate** - Customer clicks on product
- **Purchase Rate** - % who purchased after viewing
- **Return Rate** - % of returns

---

### Inventory Metrics
- **Stock Levels** - Current inventory
- **Stock Turnover** - How fast stock sells
- **Reorder Frequency** - How often restocked
- **Out of Stock Days** - Days item was unavailable
