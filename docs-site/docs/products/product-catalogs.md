# Product Catalogs

## Overview

Product Catalogs allow you to organize and categorize your products for better management and classification. Group products by category, type, brand, region, or any other organizational structure that fits your business needs.

## What are Catalogs?

Catalogs are **organizational structures** that help you:
- Group related products together
- Organize products by category or type
- Classify products by brand or region
- Make products easier to find and manage
