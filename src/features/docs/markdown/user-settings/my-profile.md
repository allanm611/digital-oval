# My Profile

## Overview

My Profile is your personal account page where you can view and edit your profile information, including personal details, professional information, and account details.

## Accessing My Profile

**Step 1: Open User Menu**
- Click your **profile icon** in the top-right corner
- A dropdown menu appears

**Step 2: Navigate to My Profile**
- Click on **"My Profile"** option
- Your profile page loads

## Profile Information

![My Profile Page](/img/usersettings/profilepage.png)

Your profile displays the following sections:

### Profile Header

At the top of the page:
- **Avatar** - Your initials displayed as a colored circle
- **Full Name** - First name and last name
- **Email Address** - Your account email
- **Status Badge** - Current account status (Active, Inactive, Suspended, etc.)
- **Role Badge** - Your assigned role in the system

### Personal Information (Editable)

Click **Edit Profile** to modify these fields:

1. **First Name** 
   - Your first name
   - Shown in your profile header
   - Example: "John"

2. **Last Name** 
   - Your family name
   - Shown in your profile header
   - Example: "Smith"

3. **Middle Name**
   - Your middle name
   - Shown in profile details
   - Example: "Michael"

4. **Preferred Name**
   - Name you prefer to be called
   - Alternative to first/last name
   - Example: "Johnny"

5. **Phone Number**
   - Your contact phone number
   - Example: "+254712345678"

6. **Department**
   - Your department or team
   - Used for organizational purposes
   - Example: "Marketing"

7. **Job Title**
   - Your position in the organization
   - Example: "Campaign Manager"

8. **Time Zone**
   - Your local time zone
   - Used for displaying timestamps
   - Example: "Africa/Nairobi"

### Account Information (Read-Only)

These details are set by administrators and cannot be changed:

- **Username** - Your login username
- **Email Address** - Cannot be changed (marked as read-only with note)
- **Role** - Your assigned role and permissions
- **Status** - Your account status
- **Data Access Level** - What data you can access
- **PII Access** - Whether you can access personally identifiable information
- **Last Login** - When you last logged in
- **Account Created** - When your account was created
- **Last Updated** - When your profile was last modified

### Important Notes

- **Email cannot be changed** - Contact your administrator if you need to update your email
- **Required fields** - First Name and Last Name must always be filled in to avoid errors
- **Optional fields** - Department, Job Title, Phone Number, Timezone, Middle Name, and Preferred Name are optional
- **Changes save immediately** - Once you click Save Changes, updates are applied to your account
