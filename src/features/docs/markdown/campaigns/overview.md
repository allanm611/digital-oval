# Campaign Management

## Overview

Campaign Management allows you to create and manage marketing campaigns. Campaigns let you target specific customer segments with offers, track execution, and monitor performance through analytics.

## Key Features

- **Campaign Management** - Create and manage marketing campaigns
- **Campaign Types** - Multiple campaign structures for different strategies
- **Campaign Catalogs** - Organize campaigns by category
- **Performance Tracking** - Monitor campaign execution and results
- **Search & Filter** - Quickly find campaigns

## Getting Started

### Viewing Campaigns
To browse all your campaigns in one place, see [Campaign List](/documentation/campaigns/campaigns-list) to view campaign status, associated offers and segments, and take quick actions.

### Creating a Campaign
To set up a new campaign, see [Create Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/create-campaign) for a complete 5-step process:
1. **Definition** - Configure campaign details, name, catalog, and business unit
2. **Campaign Configuration & Target Audience** - Select campaign type and choose customer segments to target
3. **Offers** - Set up offers for your segments
4. **Schedule** - Set execution timing
5. **Preview** - Review before launch

### Campaign Details
To see all information about a specific campaign, see [View Campaign Details](/documentation/campaigns/view-campaign-details) for status, configuration, targeted segments, offers, and performance metrics.

### Modifying Campaigns
To modify campaign details at any stage, see [Edit Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/edit-campaign) to change targeting, offers, scheduling, and other settings.

### Campaign Analytics
To track campaign performance, see [Campaign Analytics](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-reports) for detailed metrics and reports.

## Campaign Information

### Campaign Types
To understand the different campaign types available, see [Campaign Types](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-types) to learn when to use each one (Multiple Target Group, etc.).

### Campaign Objectives
To learn about campaign goals, see [Campaign Objectives](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-objectives) for objectives that can be configured.

### Campaign Catalog
To understand how campaigns are organized, see [Campaign Catalog](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-catalog) for catalog management and organization.

### Campaign Broadcasts
To send broadcast campaigns, see [Campaign Broadcasts](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-broadcasts) for sending campaigns to customer segments.

## Quick Start

**New to campaigns?**

1. Go to [Campaign List](/documentation/campaigns/campaigns-list) to see existing campaigns
2. Click on a campaign to [View Details](/documentation/campaigns/view-campaign-details)
3. Ready to create? Follow [Create Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/create-campaign) for step-by-step instructions
4. After launch, check [Campaign Analytics](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-reports) to see how your campaign is performing
