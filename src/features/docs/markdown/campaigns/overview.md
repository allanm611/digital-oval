# Campaign Management

## Overview

Campaign Management allows you to create and manage marketing campaigns. Campaigns let you target specific customer segments with offers, track execution, and monitor performance through analytics.

---

## What You Can Do

### [Campaign List](/documentation/campaigns/campaigns-list)

Browse all your campaigns in one place. View campaign status, associated offers and segments, and take quick actions like editing, viewing details, or deleting campaigns.

### [Create Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/create-campaign)

Set up a new campaign through a 5-step process:
1. **Definition** - Configure campaign details, name, catalog, and business unit
2. **Campaign Configuration & Target Audience** - Select campaign type and choose customer segments to target
3. **Offers** - Set up offers for your segments
4. **Schedule** - Set execution timing
5. **Preview** - Review before launch

### [View Campaign Details](/documentation/campaigns/view-campaign-details)

See all information about a specific campaign including status, configuration, targeted segments, offers, and performance metrics.

### [Edit Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/edit-campaign)

Modify campaign details at any stage. Change targeting, offers, scheduling, and other settings.

### [Campaign Analytics](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-reports)

Track campaign performance with detailed metrics and reports.

## Quick Start

**New to campaigns?**

1. Go to [Campaign List](/documentation/campaigns/campaigns-list) to see existing campaigns
2. Click on a campaign to [View Details](/documentation/campaigns/view-campaign-details)
3. Ready to create? Follow [Create Campaign](/documentation/campaigns/create-campaign) for step-by-step instructions
4. After launch, check [Campaign Analytics](/documentation/campaigns/campaign-reports) to see how your campaign is performing
