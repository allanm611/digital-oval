# Customer Reports

## Overview

Customer Reports provide comprehensive analytics on customer behavior, engagement, demographics, and lifecycle metrics. Analyze customer trends, measure campaign effectiveness, understand customer value, and optimize targeting strategies with detailed customer insights.

---

## Accessing Customer Reports

**Navigation:** Dashboard → Customer 360 → Customer Reports

The Customer Reports page displays your customer analytics with interactive charts, tables, and filtering options.

---

## Overview Dashboard

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**Customer Base**
- **Total Customers** - Total number of customers in system
- **Active Customers** - Customers active in last 30/60/90 days
- **Inactive Customers** - No activity for specified period
- **New Customers This Month** - Recently added customers
- **Customer Growth** - Month-over-month change

**Engagement Metrics**
- **Overall Engagement Rate** - % of customers engaged
- **Email Open Rate** - % of sent emails opened
- **Email Click Rate** - % of opens that clicked
- **SMS Response Rate** - % of SMS that got response
- **Campaign Participation** - % participating in campaigns

**Customer Value**
- **Total Revenue** - Lifetime revenue from all customers
- **Average Customer Value** - Mean customer revenue
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** - Predicted lifetime revenue
- **Repeat Purchase Rate** - % making multiple purchases
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** - Mean transaction amount

**Health Metrics**
- **Churn Rate** - % of customers becoming inactive
- **Reactivation Rate** - % of churned customers returning
- **Customer Retention Rate** - % retained period-over-period
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** - Customer satisfaction metric

## Report Types & Views

### Customer Segmentation Report

**Purpose:** Understand customer distribution across segments

**Available Data:**
- **Segment Name** - Segment name
- **Customer Count** - Total in segment
- **Growth Rate** - Segment growth %
- **Engagement Level** - Segment engagement (High/Medium/Low)
- **Avg. Customer Value** - Average value per customer
- **Total Revenue** - Total revenue from segment
- **Email Open Rate** - Segment email open rate
- **Churn Rate** - Churn within segment

**Views:**
- **Table View** - Detailed data table
- **Bar Chart** - Compare segment sizes
- **Pie Chart** - Segment distribution
- **Trend Graph** - Growth over time

**Use Cases:**
- Compare segment performance
- Identify high-value segments
- Monitor segment growth
- Track engagement by segment

### Customer Demographic Report

**Purpose:** Analyze customer makeup and characteristics

**Available Dimensions:**
- **Age Groups** - Customer age distribution (18-25, 26-35, 36-45, etc.)
- **Gender** - Distribution by gender
- **Location** - Customers by city, region, country
- **Language Preference** - Preferred language breakdown
- **Timezone** - Customer timezone distribution
- **Customer Tier** - Distribution by tier (Gold, Silver, Bronze, etc.)
- **VIP Status** - VIP vs. regular customers

**Metrics:**
- **Customer Count** - Count in each demographic
- **Percentage** - % of total customer base
- **Engagement Rate** - Engagement by demographic
- **Revenue** - Revenue by demographic

**Use Cases:**
- Demographic targeting analysis
- Regional performance analysis
- Language preference verification
- Tier distribution insights

### Customer Lifecycle Report

**Purpose:** Track customers through their journey

**Lifecycle Stages:**
- **Prospects** - Potential customers, not yet customers
- **New Customers** - First 30 days after registration
- **Active Customers** - Engaged recently
- **At-Risk** - Low activity, may churn
- **Churned** - Inactive for specified period
- **Reactivated** - Previously churned, returned

**Metrics Per Stage:**
- **Count** - Number in stage
- **% of Total** - Percentage of customer base
- **Avg. Duration** - Average time in stage
- **Next Stage %** - % moving to next stage
- **Revenue** - Revenue from stage

**Flow Visualization:**
- Waterfall showing stage progression
- Retention rates between stages
- Churn rates from each stage
- Stage-to-stage transitions

**Use Cases:**
- Monitor customer journey
- Identify churn indicators
- Plan stage-specific campaigns
- Optimize customer retention

### Communication Preference Report

**Purpose:** Analyze opt-in status and channel preferences

**Opt-in Status Breakdown:**
- **Email Opted In** - % with email enabled
- **Email Opted Out** - % with email disabled
- **SMS Opted In** - % with SMS enabled
- **SMS Opted Out** - % with SMS disabled
- **Push Opted In** - % with push enabled
- **Push Opted Out** - % with push disabled
- **All Channels Opted In** - % in all channels
- **Any Channel Opted In** - % in at least one channel

**Channel Preference Analysis:**
- **Preferred SMS** - Number preferring SMS
- **Preferred Email** - Number preferring Email
- **Preferred WhatsApp** - Number preferring WhatsApp
- **Preferred Push** - Number preferring Push
- **Preferred Other** - Other channel preferences

**DND Analysis:**
- **DND Enabled** - % with DND active
- **DND Hours** - Most common DND times
- **DND Days** - Most common DND days
- **Peak Quiet Hours** - When most customers DND active

**Compliance Metrics:**
- **Unsubscribed** - Total unsubscribes
- **Hard Bounces** - Invalid email addresses
- **Complaint Rate** - Spam complaints
- **List Churn** - Monthly opt-out rate

**Use Cases:**
- Verify compliance with customer preferences
- Plan channel-specific campaigns
- Identify low opt-in channels
- Monitor unsubscribe trends

**Top Performers:**

**Use Cases:**

### Engagement Score Report

**Purpose:** Measure overall customer engagement

**Score Components:**

- **Purchase Frequency** - Purchase activity

**Score Distribution:**
- **High Engagement** - Score 80-100

- **Low Engagement** - Score 20-49

**Metrics by Engagement Level:**

## Filtering & Customization

### Date Range

**Preset Options:**
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days (Month)
- Last 90 Days (Quarter)
- Year-to-Date
- Last 12 Months
- Custom Range

**Impact:**
- All metrics recalculate for period
- Trends show only for selected period
- Comparisons can use different ranges

### Dimension Filters

**By Segment**
- Show data for specific segment(s)
- Compare multiple segments
- Exclude segments

**By Status**
- Active Customers
- Inactive Customers
- Blocked Customers
- All Statuses

**By Tier**
- Gold, Silver, Bronze, Standard
- Single or multiple tiers
- Exclude specific tiers

**By Channel**
- SMS Only
- Email Only
- Multi-channel
- Specific channel combinations

**By Source/Acquisition**
- New customers
- Imported customers
- Organic growth
- Integration sources

**By Geographic**
- Country/Region
- City
- Timezone
- Exclude locations

### Custom Filters

Create custom filter combinations:
1\. Select first filter dimension
2\. Choose filter value
3\. Add additional filters
4\. Click **Apply Filters**
5\. Metrics update to match

## Data Export & Scheduling

### Export Options

**Export Formats**
- **CSV** - For spreadsheet analysis
- **JSON** - For system integration
- **PDF** - For sharing/reports
- **Excel** - For formatted spreadsheet

**What's Included**
- All visible data
- All calculated metrics
- Applied filters
- Date range selected
- Timestamps and metadata

### Download Reports

**Single Download**
1\. Configure report (filters, date range)
2\. Click **Export** button
3\. Select format
4\. File downloads to computer

**Scheduled Reports**

Automatically generate reports on schedule:

**Setup:**
1\. Click **Schedule Report**
2\. Choose frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
3\. Select format (CSV, PDF, etc.)
4\. Choose delivery email(s)
5\. Click **Save Schedule**

**Scheduled Delivery:**
- Reports generated at scheduled time
- Emailed to specified recipients
- Stored for download
- Can modify or cancel anytime

## Common Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Identify High-Value Customers

**Goal:** Focus marketing spend on most valuable customers

**Steps:**
1\. Go to Customer Value Report
2\. Filter by Revenue (High-Value tier)
3\. Export customer list
4\. Create VIP segment
5\. Launch exclusive campaigns

**Result:** Higher ROI, better retention

### Use Case 2: Reduce Churn

**Goal:** Identify and re-engage at-risk customers

**Steps:**
1\. Go to Customer Lifecycle Report
2\. Filter "At-Risk" stage
3\. Note characteristics (engagement score, last activity)
4\. Create re-engagement campaign
5\. Track results

**Result:** Recovered revenue, improved retention

### Use Case 3: Optimize Send Timing

**Goal:** Send campaigns when customers most likely to engage

**Steps:**
1\. Go to Campaign Engagement Report
2\. Analyze open rates by time/day
3\. Identify peak engagement times
4\. Schedule campaigns for optimal times
5\. Monitor improvement

**Result:** Higher engagement, better click rates

### Use Case 4: Channel Preference Strategy

**Goal:** Use preferred channels for better engagement

**Steps:**
1\. Go to Communication Preference Report
2\. Review opt-in rates by channel
3\. Note customer channel preferences
4\. Segment by preferred channel
5\. Send via preferred channels

**Result:** Higher engagement, lower opt-outs
