# Campaign Reports

## Overview

Campaign Reports provide detailed performance metrics for all your campaigns. Analyze campaign effectiveness, reach, engagement, conversion, and ROI across channels and time periods.

## Key Metrics

### Campaign Summary

**Hero Metrics** displayed at the top:

**Reach & Engagement**
- **Total Campaigns:** Number of campaigns in selected period
- **Active Campaigns:** Currently running campaigns
- **Total Reach:** Combined audience across campaigns
- **Total Recipients:** Unique customers reached

**Performance**
- **Engagement Rate:** Overall campaign engagement (%)
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Percentage clicking campaign content
- **Conversion Rate:** Campaign actions/conversions (%)
- **Average Campaign Cost:** Cost per campaign
- **Total ROI:** Return on investment (%)

**Channel Performance**
- **Email Campaigns:** Campaigns using email
- **SMS Campaigns:** SMS-based campaigns
- **Multi-channel:** Campaigns using 2+ channels
- **Best Channel:** Highest performing channel

## KPIs & Benchmarks

### Key Metrics Explained

**Engagement Rate**
- Percentage of recipients who interacted
- Formula: (Opens + Clicks) / Delivered × 100
- Higher is better
- Benchmark: 15-25% for email, 5-10% for SMS

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**
- Percentage clicking campaign links
- Formula: Clicks / Delivered × 100
- Indicates interest level
- Benchmark: 2-5% for email, 1-3% for SMS

**Conversion Rate**
- Percentage completing target action
- Formula: Conversions / Delivered × 100
- Measures campaign effectiveness
- Benchmark: 1-3% average

**Return on Investment (ROI)**
- Revenue generated vs cost
- Formula: (Revenue - Cost) / Cost × 100
- Measures profitability
- Target: Positive ROI

**Cost Per Conversion**
- Average cost to acquire one conversion
- Formula: Total Cost / Conversions
- Lower is better
- Optimize to reduce

## Troubleshooting

### Missing Campaign Data
- **"Campaign not showing"** - Check status filter (may be in Draft)
- **"No data for date range"** - Verify campaign ran in selected period
- **"Metrics are 0"** - Ensure campaign has recipients

### Inaccurate Metrics
- **"Numbers don't match"** - Verify filter selections
- **"Delivery rate &gt;100%"** - Contact support (data issue)
- **"Negative ROI unclear"** - Review cost configuration

### Report Issues
- **"Export fails"** - Try smaller date range
- **"Report loads slowly"** - Reduce number of campaigns filtered
- **"Charts not displaying"** - Refresh page or clear filters
