# Analytics

## Overview

Analytics provides comprehensive reporting and insights into your customer engagement platform's performance. Track campaign effectiveness, customer behavior, delivery metrics, and business KPIs to measure success and optimize your strategies.

---

## What You Can Do

### [Overall Dashboard Performance](/documentation/analytics/overall-dashboard-performance)

View comprehensive metrics across your entire platform. Monitor customer metrics, revenue metrics, campaign performance, and delivery statistics in one unified dashboard.

### [Campaign Reports](/documentation/analytics/campaign-reports)

Track campaign performance with detailed metrics including reach, conversion rates, revenue impact, and engagement metrics. Analyze individual campaign results and compare campaign performance.

### [Offer Reports](/documentation/analytics/offer-reports)

Monitor offer performance and redemption metrics. Track which offers drive the most engagement and revenue, and measure offer effectiveness across segments.

### [Customer Profile Reports](/documentation/analytics/customer-profile-reports)

Analyze customer data and insights. Track customer acquisition, retention, lifetime value, and segmentation patterns.

### [Delivery Reports](/documentation/analytics/delivery-sms-reports)

Monitor delivery performance across channels. Track SMS delivery success, email open rates, and delivery metrics by channel and campaign.

### [KPIs](/documentation/analytics/kpis)

Understand key performance indicators and business metrics available in the system. Learn how to measure and interpret platform performance.

---

## Quick Start

**New to analytics?**

1. Go to [Overall Dashboard Performance](/documentation/analytics/overall-dashboard-performance) for a comprehensive overview
2. Check [Campaign Reports](/documentation/analytics/campaign-reports) to track your campaigns
3. Review [Offer Reports](/documentation/analytics/offer-reports) to see offer performance
4. Monitor [Customer Profile Reports](/documentation/analytics/customer-profile-reports) for customer insights
5. Track [Delivery Reports](/documentation/analytics/delivery-sms-reports) to monitor message delivery
