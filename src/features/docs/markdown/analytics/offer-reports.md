# Offer Reports

## Overview

Offer Reports track the performance of your promotional offers, discounts, and deals. Analyze offer effectiveness, redemption rates, and ROI to optimize your promotional strategy.

## Key Metrics

### Hero Metrics

**Offer Performance at a Glance:**

**Offer Statistics**
- **Total Offers:** Active offers in period
- **Active Offers:** Currently running
- **Expired Offers:** No longer valid
- **Offers Created:** New offers in period

**Redemption**
- **Total Redemptions:** Number of times redeemed
- **Redemption Rate:** % of distributed offers redeemed
- **Average Redemption:** Average per offer
- **Unredeemed Rate:** % not redeemed

**Financial**
- **Total Offer Value:** Total worth of offers
- **Revenue Generated:** From redeemed offers
- **Average Offer Value:** Value per offer
- **ROI:** Return on offer investment

**Engagement**
- **Offers Distributed:** Offers sent/shown
- **Unique Recipients:** Customers receiving offers
- **Engagement Rate:** % interacting with offer
- **Conversion Rate:** % redeeming offer
