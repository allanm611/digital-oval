# Offer

## Overview

The Offer section is your hub for managing all customer offers and promotional deals. Create, configure, and track offers that you can assign to campaigns and deliver to customer segments.

## Key Features

- **Offer Management** - Create and manage promotional offers
- **Offer Catalogs** - Organize offers into catalogs
- **Performance Tracking** - Monitor offer performance and redemption
- **Campaign Integration** - Assign offers to campaigns
- **Search & Filter** - Quickly find offers

## Offer Status

- **Draft** - Offer being created
- **Active** - Offer available for campaigns
- **Paused** - Temporarily disabled
- **Archived** - Old offers for reference
- **Expired** - Offer end date passed


## Tips & Best Practices

1. **Clear Offer Names** - Use descriptive names
2. **Set Realistic Limits** - Define budget and quantity limits
3. **Monitor Redemption** - Track offer performance regularly
4. **Archive Old Offers** - Keep your list organized
5. **Test Offers** - Verify impact before full rollout
