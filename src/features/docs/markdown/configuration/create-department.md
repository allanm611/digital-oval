# Create Department

## Overview

Create a new Department to represent an organizational unit within your company. Departments help organize teams, assign campaigns, and track performance across your organization.

## How to Create a Department

### Step 1: Open the Creation Form

1. Navigate to **Configuration → Departments**
2. Click the **Create** button at the top
3. A modal dialog opens with the creation form

### Step 2: Fill in Department Information

#### Department Name (Required)
- **Field Type:** Text input
- **Max Length:** 100 characters
- **Description:** Unique name for the department
- **Examples:**
  - "Marketing"
  - "Sales"
  - "Customer Support"
  - "Product Management"
  - "Finance"
  - "Operations"
  - "IT"
  - "Human Resources"

#### Description (Optional)
- **Field Type:** Text area
- **Max Length:** 500 characters
- **Description:** Explain the department's role and responsibilities
- **Examples:**
  - "Responsible for marketing campaigns and customer acquisition"
  - "Handles sales operations and customer relationships"
  - "Provides customer service and technical support"
  - "Manages product development and strategy"

### Step 3: Save the Department

1. Review the entered information
2. Click the **Save** or **Create** button
3. System validates:
   - Name is provided and unique
   - Character limits are respected
4. Upon success:
   - Confirmation message appears
   - Modal closes
   - Department appears in the list

## Validation Rules

- **Department name is required**
- **Name must be unique** - No duplicate names
- **Character limits:**
  - Name: Maximum 100 characters
  - Description: Maximum 500 characters

## Department Types

### Operational Departments
- **Marketing** - Campaign creation and management
- **Sales** - Sales and customer relationship
- **Customer Support** - Customer service
- **Product Management** - Product strategy

### Support Departments
- **Finance** - Budget and financial operations
- **IT** - Technology and infrastructure
- **HR** - Human resources
- **Operations** - General operations

## Naming Guidelines

- Be specific about department function
- Use consistent naming with organization
- Avoid abbreviations unless widely known
- Examples:
  - "Digital Marketing" not "DM"
  - "Enterprise Sales" not "E-Sales"
  - "Technical Support" not "TS"

## Best Practices

- Create departments matching actual organization
- Avoid creating too many departments
- Document department responsibilities
- Align with company structure
- Plan future organizational changes
