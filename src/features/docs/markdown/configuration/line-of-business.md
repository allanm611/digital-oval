# Line of Business Overview

Line of Business represents distinct business units, divisions, or customer segments within your organization. They help structure your campaigns, manage business-specific strategies, and organize customer communications by business area.

## What is Line of Business?

Line of Business (LOB) structures are:
- **Business Divisions** - Represent distinct business units
- **Customer Segments** - Organize by customer type or segment
- **Product Lines** - Group by product or service categories
- **Geographic Regions** - Organize by market or region
- **Campaign Groupings** - Organize campaigns by business area

## Purpose & Benefits

### Why Use Line of Business?

**Business Organization**
- Separate campaigns by business division
- Organize by product lines or customer segments
- Manage business-specific strategies
- Align campaigns with business units

**Operational Efficiency**
- Delegate campaigns to business units
- Business unit-specific workflows
- Clear ownership and accountability
- Streamlined campaign management

**Performance Tracking**
- Monitor metrics by business unit
- Compare performance across LOBs
- Budget allocation by LOB
- ROI analysis by business division

**Scalability**
- Support multiple business units
- Independent LOB management
- Centralized platform
- Flexible organization structure

### Key Benefits

- **Organization:** Clear business unit structure
- **Ownership:** Assign campaigns to specific LOBs
- **Accountability:** Track performance by LOB
- **Flexibility:** Customize LOBs for your business model
- **Reporting:** LOB-level analytics and insights
- **Efficiency:** Streamlined multi-LOB management

## Managing Line of Business

### Viewing Line of Business List

Navigate to **Configuration → Line of Business** to see all configured business lines.

![Line of Business List](/img/configuration/lineofbusinesslist.png)

### Create Line of Business

Click the **Create** button to add a new line of business.

![Create Line of Business](/img/configuration/createlineofbusiness.png)

**LOB Configuration:**
1. **Line of Business Name** (Required)
   - Descriptive name for the business unit
   - Examples: "Mobile Services", "Enterprise Solutions", "Retail"
   - Max 100 characters

2. **Description** (Optional)
   - Explain the business unit's focus
   - Target customers and products/services
   - Geographic or market focus
   - Max 500 characters

3. **Business Type** (Optional)
   - Categorize the LOB type (Product, Geographic, Segment, Channel, etc.)
   - Helps organize and identify similar LOBs
   - Enables filtering and reporting

4. **Parent LOB** (Optional)
   - Create hierarchical LOB structures
   - Sub-units of larger business areas
   - Enables nested organization

5. **Status** (Optional)
   - Set LOB as Active or Inactive
   - Inactive LOBs are archived but retained
   - Can be reactivated later

### Edit Line of Business

Click the **Edit** button on any LOB to update:
- Business unit name
- Description
- Business type
- Parent LOB
- Status

### Delete Line of Business

Click the **Delete** button to remove a LOB:
- LOB is permanently removed
- Campaigns assigned to the LOB are not deleted
- Verify no active campaigns use this LOB before deleting

## Best Practices

### LOB Design

1. **Clear Definition** - Define each LOB's scope and responsibility
2. **Meaningful Names** - Use clear names that describe the business area
3. **Documented Scope** - Document what's included in each LOB
4. **Logical Structure** - Create coherent LOB hierarchy
5. **Regular Review** - Periodically review LOB structure

### Campaign Organization

1. **Single LOB Assignment** - Assign each campaign to one primary LOB
2. **Clear Ownership** - Each LOB clearly owns their campaigns
3. **Consistent Naming** - Use consistent LOB references in campaigns
4. **Avoid Overlap** - Don't create redundant or overlapping LOBs
5. **Track Lifecycle** - Monitor campaigns from creation to completion

### Maintenance

1. **Regular Updates** - Keep LOB descriptions current
2. **Remove Unused** - Delete obsolete LOBs
3. **Monitor Usage** - Review which LOBs are actively used
4. **Align with Organization** - Keep LOB structure aligned with actual business structure
5. **Team Communication** - Inform stakeholders of LOB changes

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Create Line of Business

**Issue:** "LOB creation failed" error

**Solutions:**
- Verify LOB name is not empty
- Check name doesn't exceed 100 characters
- Verify name is unique (no duplicates)
- Ensure you have permission to create LOBs

### Cannot Delete Line of Business

**Issue:** "Cannot delete LOB - campaigns exist" error

**Solutions:**
- View all campaigns assigned to this LOB
- Delete or reassign campaigns to different LOB
- Then retry LOB deletion
- Alternatively, deactivate LOB instead of deleting

### Hierarchical Structure Issues

**Issue:** Cannot create sub-LOBs or circular references

**Solutions:**
- Ensure parent LOB is not a sub-LOB of child LOB
- Select valid parent LOB only
- Avoid circular LOB references
- Test hierarchy before creating campaigns

### Missing Line of Business

**Issue:** Expected LOB doesn't appear in list

**Solutions:**
- Check if LOB is inactive (filter to show inactive)
- Verify LOB name spelling
- Search by LOB description
- Check LOB creation was saved
