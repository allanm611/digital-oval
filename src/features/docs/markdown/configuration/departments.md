# Departments Overview

Departments represent organizational units within your company. They help structure your team, organize campaigns, assign responsibilities, and track performance across different business functions.

## What are Departments?

Departments are:
- **Organizational Units** - Represent teams or divisions in your company
- **Campaign Owners** - Campaigns can be assigned to departments
- **Team Groupings** - Team members assigned to departments
- **Accountability Centers** - Track metrics by department
- **Permission Scopes** - Control access and approvals

## Default Departments

Your system includes five pre-configured departments:

### Marketing
- Responsible for campaigns and customer acquisition
- Focuses on audience targeting and messaging
- Creates awareness and conversion campaigns
- Tracks CAC and conversion metrics

### Sales
- Handles sales operations and relationships
- Focuses on lead nurturing and sales enablement
- Creates sales-driven campaigns
- Tracks deal closure and pipeline metrics

### Customer Support
- Provides customer service and support
- Focuses on satisfaction and issue resolution
- Creates support and engagement campaigns
- Tracks satisfaction and resolution metrics

### Product Management
- Manages product development and strategy
- Focuses on feature launches and adoption
- Creates product education campaigns
- Tracks adoption and user feedback

### Finance
- Handles financial operations and budgets
- Focuses on billing and payments
- Creates billing and financial campaigns
- Tracks collection and budget metrics

## Best Practices

### Department Setup
- Align with actual organization structure
- Use clear, descriptive names
- Document department responsibilities
- Define department goals and metrics

### Campaign Management

- Coordinate between departments
- Enable department approval workflows
- Track campaign ownership

### Monitoring & Reporting
- Review campaign performance by department
- Track budget allocation
- Monitor team productivity
- Evaluate department effectiveness

### Growth Planning
- Plan for organizational changes
- Consider future departments
- Document department evolution
- Maintain consistent naming
