# Create Campaign Objective

## Overview

Create a new Campaign Objective to define a strategic goal or business outcome for your marketing campaigns. Objectives help organize, categorize, and track campaigns by their intended purpose.

## How to Create an Objective

### Step 1: Open the Creation Form

1. Navigate to **Configuration → Campaign Objectives**
2. Click the **Create** button at the top
3. A modal dialog opens with the creation form

![Create Campaign Objective](/img/configuration/createcampaignobjective.png)

### Step 2: Fill in Objective Information

#### Objective Name (Required)
- **Field Type:** Text input
- **Max Length:** 100 characters
- **Description:** Unique name for the objective
- **Examples:**
  - "New Customer Acquisition"
  - "Customer Retention"
  - "Churn Prevention"
  - "Upsell/Cross-sell"
  - "Dormant Customer Reactivation"

#### Description (Optional)
- **Field Type:** Text area
- **Max Length:** 500 characters
- **Description:** Explain the objective's purpose
- **Examples:**
  - "Attract and convert new customers to your service"
  - "Keep existing customers engaged and loyal"
  - "Increase revenue from existing customers"

### Step 3: Save the Objective

1. Review the entered information
2. Click the **Save** or **Create** button
3. System validates:
   - Name is provided and unique
   - Character limits are respected
4. Upon success:
   - Confirmation message appears
   - Modal closes
   - Objective appears in the list

## Validation Rules

- **Objective name is required**
- **Name must be unique** - No duplicate names
- **Character limits:**
  - Name: Maximum 100 characters
  - Description: Maximum 500 characters

## Naming Guidelines

Create clear, descriptive objective names:
- Be specific about the goal
- Avoid jargon or abbreviations
- Use consistent naming patterns
- Examples of good names:
  - "Mobile App Download Promotion"
  - "VIP Customer Exclusive Offer"
  - "Annual Subscription Upsell"

## Best Practices

### Objective Creation
- Create objectives aligned with business strategy
- Avoid creating too many similar objectives
- Document the purpose and scope
- Think about measurement before creating

### Organization
- Group related objectives logically
- Use consistent naming conventions
- Create objectives for major business goals
- Plan objectives before campaign creation

### Future Planning
- Consider objectives you'll use repeatedly
- Create objectives for each business unit if needed
- Anticipate future campaign types
- Keep list manageable (5-20 objectives)
