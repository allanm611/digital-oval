# View Department Details

## Overview

View detailed information about a Department including its name, description, assigned team members, and campaigns.

## How to View Department Details

### From the List View
1. Navigate to **Configuration → Departments**
2. Click on any department name in the list
3. The details page opens

### From Search Results
1. Use the search field to find a department
2. Click on the matching department
3. Details page loads

## Department Details Page

### Basic Information

**Department Name**
- Unique identifier for the department
- Used in campaign and team assignment
- Examples: "Marketing", "Sales", "Customer Support"

**Description**
- Detailed explanation of department role
- Documents responsibilities and focus
- Helps team understand department purpose

**Created/Updated Dates**
- When department was created
- Most recent modification

### Related Information

**Team Members**
- Count of members in department
- List of assigned team members
- Member roles and responsibilities

**Active Campaigns**
- Campaigns assigned to department
- Campaign status
- Campaign performance metrics

**Campaign History**
- Campaigns managed by department
- Total campaigns
- Performance trends

## Available Actions

### Edit Department
- Click **Edit** button to modify
- Update name or description
- See [Edit Department](/documentation/edit-department)

### Delete Department
- Click **Delete** button to remove
- Cannot delete if actively in use
- Permanent action

### View Team Members
- Click on team members section
- See assigned personnel
- View member details

### View Campaigns
- See campaigns by department
- Click campaign for details
- View campaign performance

## Department Performance

### Metrics by Department

**Marketing**
- Campaign count
- Total reach
- Conversion rate

**Sales**

- Deal closure rate
- Sales pipeline value
- Revenue impact

**Customer Support**
- Support campaigns sent
- Customer satisfaction

**Product**
- Product launches
- Feature announcements
