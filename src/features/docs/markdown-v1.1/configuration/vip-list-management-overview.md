# VIP List Management

## Overview

VIP List Management allows you to organize and manage your most valuable customers by creating VIP lists and adding customers to them. You can then reference these VIP lists in communication policies to provide special treatment or exclusions for your high-value customers.

## What are VIP Lists?

VIP Lists allow you to:

- **Create VIP Tiers** - Define named lists for different VIP customer segments
- **Add Customers** - Assign customers to specific VIP lists
- **Track VIP Status** - Monitor which customers are in which VIP lists
- **Use in Policies** - Reference VIP lists in communication policies for special handling

![VIP List Management Tabs](/img/v1.1/configuration/vipliststabimage.png)

- **Two-Tab Interface**: VIP Customers tab and VIP Lists tab
- **Search and Filtering**: Find customers and lists quickly
- **Member Management**: Add and remove members from VIP lists
- **Bulk Operations**: Add multiple customers at once
- **List Details**: View member counts and import/failure statistics
- **Status Tracking**: Monitor active and inactive VIP customers

![VIP List Management Overview](/img/v1.1/configuration/viplistmanagentvipliststab.png)
_VIP Lists tab showing all VIP lists and actions._

To manage VIP lists and customers:

1. Navigate to **Configuration → VIP List Management**
2. Switch between the **VIP Customers** and **VIP Lists** tabs
3. Create new VIP lists as needed
4. Add customers to VIP lists
5. Monitor and manage your VIP customer segments
