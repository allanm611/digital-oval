# Reports & Analytics

## Overview

Reports & Analytics provides comprehensive reporting and insights into your customer engagement platform's performance. Track campaign effectiveness, customer behavior, delivery metrics, and business KPIs to measure success and optimize your strategies.

## What You Can Do

### [Overall Dashboard](/documentation/reports/overall-dashboard)

View comprehensive metrics across your entire platform. Monitor channel performance, delivery statistics, and key metrics for SMS and email in one unified dashboard.

### [Campaign Reports](/documentation/reports/campaign-reports)

Track campaign performance with detailed metrics including reach, conversion rates, revenue impact, and engagement metrics. Analyze individual campaign results and compare campaign performance.

### [Offer Reports](/documentation/reports/offer-reports)

Monitor offer performance and redemption metrics. Track which offers drive the most engagement and revenue, and measure offer effectiveness across segments.

### [Customer Profile Reports](/documentation/reports/customer-profile-reports)

Analyze customer data and insights. Track customer acquisition, retention, lifetime value, and segmentation patterns.

### [SMS Delivery Reports](/documentation/reports/sms-delivery-reports)

Monitor SMS delivery performance across campaigns. Track delivery success rates, bounce rates, and message performance by campaign.

### [Email Delivery Reports](/documentation/reports/delivery-email-reports)

Monitor email delivery performance across campaigns. Track open rates, click rates, delivery metrics, and email performance by campaign.


## Quick Start

**New to reports?**

1. Go to [Overall Dashboard](/documentation/reports/overall-dashboard) for a comprehensive overview
2. Check [Campaign Reports](/documentation/reports/campaign-reports) to track your campaigns
3. Review [Offer Reports](/documentation/reports/offer-reports) to see offer performance
4. Monitor [Customer Profile Reports](/documentation/reports/customer-profile-reports) for customer insights
5. Track [SMS Delivery Reports](/documentation/reports/sms-delivery-reports) to monitor SMS performance
6. Review [Email Delivery Reports](/documentation/reports/delivery-email-reports) for email performance
