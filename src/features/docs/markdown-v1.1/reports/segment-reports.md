# Segment Reports

## Overview

Segment Reports provide comprehensive analytics on segment performance, engagement, campaign impact, and customer composition. Use these insights to optimize targeting, improve segment health, and drive better campaign results.

## Data Mode

**Toggle Data Mode:**

- Switch between live data and sample/test data
- "Data Mode" toggle in top-right corner

## Accessing Segment Reports

1. Go to **Reports** in the main navigation
2. Select **Segment Reports**
3. The dashboard loads with segment analytics, charts, and tables

## Key Metrics Overview

![Segment Size Metrics](/img/v1.1/reports/segmentreportsimage1.png)

Four key metrics provide a snapshot of segment health:

### 1. Total Customers

**What it shows:**

- The total number of customers in the segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Indicates segment reach and potential campaign impact
- Useful for comparing segment sizes

### 2. Active Customers

**What it shows:**

- Number of customers actively engaged in recent campaigns
  **Why it matters:**
- Reveals segment engagement and quality
- High active count = healthy, responsive segment

### 3. Inactive Customers

**What it shows:**

- Customers in the segment who have not engaged recently
  **Why it matters:**
- Identifies dormant or at-risk customers
- Opportunity for reactivation campaigns

### 4. Growth Rate

**What it shows:**

- Change in segment size over time
  **Why it matters:**
- Tracks acquisition or churn trends
- Helps spot emerging or shrinking segments

## Performance & Campaign Metrics

![Campaign Performance](/img/v1.1/reports/segmentreportsimage2.png)

### 5. Engagement Rate

**What it shows:**

- Percentage of segment members who engaged with recent campaigns
  **Why it matters:**
- Core measure of segment responsiveness
- Low engagement = review targeting or content

### 6. Conversion Rate

**What it shows:**

- Percentage of segment members who completed a desired action (e.g., purchase, signup)
  **Why it matters:**
- Indicates segment value and campaign effectiveness
- Use to compare segments or campaigns

### 7. Revenue

**What it shows:**

- Total revenue generated from the segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Direct business impact of the segment
- Use to prioritize high-value segments

### 8. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**What it shows:**

- Average long-term value of customers in the segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Identifies segments with high retention and profitability
- Guides investment and retention strategy

### 9. Campaigns Run

**What it shows:**

- Number of campaigns targeting the segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Shows marketing focus and testing frequency
- Use to balance campaign load across segments

### 10. Campaign Response

**What it shows:**

- How the segment responded to recent campaigns (open, click, conversion rates)
  **Why it matters:**
- Reveals which campaigns resonate best
- Guides future content and targeting

### 11. Best Performing Campaign

**What it shows:**

- The campaign with the highest impact for this segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Use as a model for future campaigns
- Learn what works for this audience

### 12. Average Performance

**What it shows:**

- Typical results for campaigns targeting this segment
  **Why it matters:**
- Sets a performance baseline
- Helps spot outliers (good or bad)

## Demographic Breakdown

View the composition of each segment to tailor messaging and offers:

- **Age Distribution**: See age groups within the segment
- **Geographic**: Understand where members are located
- **Income Level**: Identify economic tiers
- **Customer Tier**: Spot premium or loyalty members


## Filtering & Analysis

Use the dashboard tools to:

- Filter by date range to analyze trends
- Compare multiple segments side by side
- Drill down by campaign to see segment-specific results
- View trends over time for growth, engagement, and revenue
