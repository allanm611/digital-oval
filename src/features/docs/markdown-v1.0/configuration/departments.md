# Departments Overview

Departments represent organizational units within your company. They help structure your team, organize campaigns and  assign responsibilities.

## What are Departments?

Departments are:
- **Organizational Units** - Represent teams or divisions in your company
- **Campaign Owners** - Campaigns can be assigned to departments
- **Team Groupings** - Team members assigned to departments
<!-- - **Accountability Centers** - Track metrics by department
- **Permission Scopes** - Control access and approvals -->


## Managing Departments

### View Departments List

Navigate to **Configuration → Departments** to see all configured departments.

![Departments List](/img/v1.0/configuration/departmentslist.png)

### Create Department

Click the **Create** button to add a new department.

![Create Department](/img/v1.0/configuration/createdepartment.png)

## Using Departments in Campaigns

When creating a campaign:

1. Go to **Create Campaign** → **Step 1: Definition**
2. Select **Department** from the dropdown
3. Choose the department responsible for the campaign
