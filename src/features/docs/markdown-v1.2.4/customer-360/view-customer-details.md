# View Customer Details

## Overview

The customer details page provides a comprehensive view of an individual customer profile. The information is organized into 14 tabs, each displaying different aspects of the customer's engagement, activity, and account information. You can navigate between tabs using the tab bar at the top of the page, and each tab provides filters, search capabilities, and actions relevant to that data.

## Page Header and Actions

The customer details page includes action buttons in the header that allow you to manage the customer:

![Customer details header](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsheader.png)


### Edit Customer

Click the **Edit** button to update customer information such as name, contact details, preferences, and custom attributes. The edit form contains all editable customer profile fields and allows you to modify any information. Changes are applied immediately upon saving.

### Delete Customer

Click the **Delete** button to permanently remove the customer record from the system. This action cannot be undone and will delete all associated data including profile information, communication history, and custom attributes. A confirmation dialog will appear before deletion is executed.

### Send Communication

Click the **Send Communication** button to send a message (SMS, Email, or other channels) directly to this customer.

![Send Communication Modal](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/sendcommunciaitonmodalcustomer.png)

**Steps:**
1. Click **Send Communication** button
2. Select the communication channel (SMS, Email, Push, etc.)
3. Select a message template or create custom content
4. Configure delivery options (schedule, personalization, language)
5. Review recipient information
6. Click **Send** to execute

**Use Cases:**
- Send urgent notifications to the customer
- Test message content before campaign deployment
- Send personalized follow-up messages
- Notify customer of special offers or updates

## Tabs

### 1. Customer Information

Displays all core customer profile details organized into logical sections for easy reference.

![Customer Information Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailscustomerinfotab.png)

**Profile Sections:**
- **Identity Information:** Customer ID, name, gender, and date of birth
- **Contact Information:** Phone number(s), email address(es), and preferred communication channel and language
- **Address Information:** Full address details including city, region, postal code, country, and street address
- **Account Details:** Customer tier classification, timezone, account activation date, and SIM-related information if applicable

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Section:**

![Customer KPIs Table](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailscustomerinfokpis.png)

Below the basic profile information, you'll find a comprehensive KPIs table that tracks customer behavior and value metrics. The KPIs are organized into categories:
- **Revenue KPIs:** Includes metrics like Lifetime Value (total revenue generated by the customer), Monthly Revenue (revenue in the current month), ARPU (average revenue per user), and Bundle Purchases (number of bundled products purchased)
- **Usage KPIs:** Includes metrics like Voice Minutes (monthly usage), SMS Volume (number of SMS sent/received monthly), Data Usage (monthly data consumption), and Active Days (number of days the customer was active in the month)

Each KPI row displays the metric name, category, current value, trend indicator (↑ up, ↓ down, → stable) with percentage change, and a brief description. Click the eye icon to view detailed information about any KPI in an expanded modal that shows current value, category, trend analysis, and a detailed breakdown of what that metric means for the customer.

**Search and Filter:**
Use the search bar above the KPIs table to find specific metrics by name or category (e.g., search "revenue" to see all revenue-related KPIs).

### 2. Events

Shows a chronological record of all customer interactions and communications. This tab helps you track every touchpoint between your system and the customer across all channels.

![Events Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailseventstab.png)

**Event Information Displayed:**
- **Event Title:** The name of the communication (e.g., "Welcome Email", "Promotional SMS", "New Arrivals Notification")
- **Description:** Details about what was communicated (e.g., "Special offers just for you")
- **Channel:** The delivery channel used (Email, SMS, or Push Notification)
- **Status:** Current delivery status including Sent, Delivered, Opened, Clicked, or Read

**Filtering and Search:**
- **Search:** Find events by title or keywords
- **Channel Filter:** View only Email, SMS, Push notifications, or All Channels
- **Status Filter:** Filter by delivery status (Sent, Delivered, Opened, Clicked, Read, or All Status)
- **Date Range:** Use the "From Date" and "To Date" fields to view events within a specific time period

Use these filters together to find specific communications. For example, filter by "Email" channel and "Opened" status to see which emails the customer opened.

### 3. Subscribed Lists

Displays all the opt-in communication lists and subscription lists that the customer has subscribed to. This helps you understand which communication preferences the customer has enabled.

![Subscribed Lists Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailssubscribedliststab.png)

**List Information:**
- **List Name:** The name of the subscription list (e.g., "Newsletter", "Promotions", "Product Updates")
- **Subscription Date:** When the customer subscribed to this list
- **Current Status:** Whether the subscription is active or if the customer has unsubscribed

**Use Cases:**
View which communication lists the customer is interested in to understand their communication preferences and personalize future outreach. If a customer has unsubscribed from a list, it will be shown with an unsubscribed status, helping you respect their preferences.

### 4. Analytics

Provides visual engagement analytics and metrics to understand the customer's interaction patterns across all channels over time.

![Analytics Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsanalyticstab.png)

**Visualizations Include:**
- **Event Distribution by Channel:** A bar chart showing how many events (communications) were sent to the customer through each channel (Email, SMS, Push). This helps identify which channel is most used for this customer
- **Activity Timeline:** A monthly breakdown showing the number of events sent to the customer each month, helping you identify peak engagement periods and seasonal patterns
- **Status Distribution:** A chart showing the proportion of events with different statuses (Sent, Delivered, Opened, Clicked, Read), giving insight into how engaged the customer is
- **Engagement Rate by Channel:** A bar chart displaying the engagement rate (percentage of communications opened/interacted with) for each channel, helping you identify which channels are most effective for this customer

**Interpretation:**
These analytics help you understand the customer's engagement level, preferred communication channels, and how responsive they are to different types of communications. Use this data to optimize future campaign strategies for this customer.

### 5. Segments

Lists all the customer segments that this customer currently belongs to, showing their segmentation across your targeting segments.

![Segments Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsegemntstab.png)

**Segment Information:**
- **Segment Name:** The name of the segment (e.g., "High Value Customers", "Frequent Buyers")
- **Segment Type:** Whether the segment is Dynamic (rules-based, membership updates automatically) or Static (manually managed)
- **Added Date:** When the customer was added to the segment

You can remove the customer from a segment if needed, which updates their membership status immediately. Note that dynamic segments may re-add the customer if they still match the segment criteria. -->

### 6. Offers

Shows all the offers that have been sent to or applied for by the customer, with their current status and redemption details.

![Offers Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsoffertab.png)

**Offer Information:**
- **Offer Name:** The name or title of the promotional offer
- **Offer Type:** The category of offer (Discount, Cashback, Voucher, Bundle, Loyalty Reward, etc.)
- **Current Status:** Whether the offer is Active (available for redemption) or Redeemed (customer has already used it)
- **Offer Value:** The monetary or percentage value of the offer
- **Redemption Date:** The date the customer redeemed the offer (if applicable)

**Use Cases:**
Review what offers have been presented to the customer, which ones they've taken advantage of, and which are still available. This helps you understand the customer's response to different types of incentives and tailor future offers accordingly.

### 7. QuickLists

Displays all the QuickLists (manually created customer lists for targeted campaigns) that this customer is a member of.

![QuickLists Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsquickliststab.png)

**QuickList Information:**
- **List Name:** The name of the QuickList
- **List Description:** Purpose or context for the QuickList (e.g., "VIP customers for pilot program")
- **Date Added:** When the customer was added to the QuickList

**Use Cases:**
QuickLists are useful for understanding which special groups or test audiences the customer belongs to. This is helpful if you're running targeted pilot programs, internal testing, or special customer groups.

### 8. Campaigns

Shows all campaigns that the customer has been included in, either as a target recipient or participant.

![Campaigns Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailscampaignstab.png)

**Campaign Information:**
- **Campaign Name:** The name of the campaign
- **Campaign Type:** The type of campaign (e.g., promotional, informational, engagement)
- **Participation Date:** When the customer was added to or participated in the campaign
- **Campaign Status:** Whether the campaign is Active, Completed, or Paused

**Use Cases:**
Review the customer's campaign history to understand their exposure to marketing initiatives, how frequently they receive campaigns, and which campaigns they've been part of. This helps identify potential campaign fatigue or engagement patterns.

### 9. Communications

Displays the customer's communication history and their communication preferences, including their opt-in/opt-out status for different communication channels.

![Communications Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailscommunicationstab.png)

**Communication History:**
- **Sent Communications:** A record of all messages sent to the customer
- **Channel Status:** Shows the status of the customer's preference for each communication channel (Email, SMS, Push, etc.)

**Preferences Section:**
- **Opt-in Status:** Shows which channels the customer has opted in to receive communications from
- **Do Not Disturb (DND):** Indicates if the customer has requested to not receive certain types of communications
- **Preferred Channel:** The customer's stated preference for how they'd like to receive communications

**Use Cases:**
Before sending communications, check this tab to ensure you're respecting the customer's preferences and complying with their opt-in/opt-out choices. Always verify that a customer has opted in to a channel before sending communications through it.

### 10. Purchase History

Shows a complete record of all purchases made by the customer, providing insight into their buying behavior and patterns.

![Purchase History Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailspurchasehistorytab.png)

**Purchase Information:**
- **Purchase Date:** When the purchase was made
- **Product/Bundle:** What was purchased
- **Purchase Amount:** The monetary value of the purchase
- **Purchase Status:** Whether the transaction was completed, pending, or cancelled
- **Payment Method:** How the purchase was paid for

**Use Cases:**
Analyze purchase history to identify the customer's favorite products, spending patterns, frequency of purchases, and average order value. This information helps you make personalized product recommendations and timing for future offers.

### 11. Loyalty & Rewards

Displays the customer's participation in your loyalty program, including points earned, redeemed rewards, and program tier status.

![Loyalty & Rewards Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsloyaly&rewardstab.png)

**Loyalty Information:**
- **Loyalty Program Status:** Whether the customer is an active member, inactive, or pending enrollment
- **Loyalty Points:** Current balance of loyalty/reward points
- **Points Earned:** Total points earned historically
- **Points Redeemed:** Total points the customer has spent
- **Program Tier:** The customer's tier level (e.g., Silver, Gold, Platinum) if your program uses tiered levels
- **Tier Benefits:** Any special benefits or privileges associated with their current tier

**Redemption History:**
View rewards the customer has redeemed, including the points spent and the reward received.

**Use Cases:**
Use this information to understand the customer's engagement with your loyalty program, encourage higher engagement through exclusive tier benefits, and identify opportunities to drive repeat purchases through the loyalty program.

### 12. Preferences

Shows the customer's personal preferences, notification settings, and communication preferences configured in their profile.

![Preferences Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailspreferencestab.png)

**Preference Categories:**
- **Communication Preferences:** Selected channels and frequency for receiving marketing communications
- **Notification Settings:** Preferences for transactional notifications (order updates, delivery notifications, etc.)
- **Privacy Settings:** Any data sharing or personalization preferences the customer has set
- **Content Preferences:** Categories of content or offers the customer is interested in receiving (e.g., news, products, seasonal promotions)

**Customization:**
Some preferences may be editable from this view depending on your configuration and the customer's permissions. Changes made here take effect immediately.

**Use Cases:**
Always check customer preferences before crafting personalized communications to ensure you're sending relevant content. Respect these preferences to maintain customer trust and engagement.

### 13. Interactions

Displays a history of direct interactions with the customer, including support tickets, feedback, complaints, and engagement activities.

![Interactions Tab](/img/v1.1/customer360-images/customerdetailsinterecationstab.png)

**Interaction Information:**
- **Interaction Date:** When the interaction occurred
- **Interaction Type:** The category of interaction (e.g., support request, feedback submission, complaint, inquiry)
- **Description:** Details about what the interaction was about
- **Resolution Status:** Whether the interaction was resolved or is still pending
- **Notes:** Any additional context or notes about the interaction

**Use Cases:**
Review interaction history to understand customer satisfaction, support needs, and recurring issues. This helps you proactively address problems and identify opportunities to improve customer experience.

### 14. Account & Device

Shows technical account information and device details associated with the customer's account, useful for understanding their usage environment and troubleshooting.

**Account Information:**
- **Account ID:** Unique identifier for the customer account
- **Account Status:** Whether the account is Active, Suspended, or Closed
- **Last Login:** When the customer last accessed their account
- **Account Created Date:** When the account was first created

**Device Information:**
- **Primary Device:** The main device the customer uses (phone model, OS, version)
- **Device Type:** Mobile, Tablet, Web, etc.
- **Last Activity Device:** The device used in the most recent activity
- **Device Registration History:** List of all devices that have accessed the account

**Security:**
This tab is useful for security and support purposes. If a customer reports access issues, you can check this tab to verify their devices and recent activity. You can also track if unusual devices have accessed the account.

**Use Cases:**
Use this information for troubleshooting technical issues, verifying device-related problems, and understanding the customer's usage environment. For security purposes, review this tab if there are suspicious account activities.
