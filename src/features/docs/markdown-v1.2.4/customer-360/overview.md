# Customer 360

## Overview

Customer 360 provides a unified view of your entire customer base with comprehensive profiles, behavioral data, and engagement history. Access unified customer profiles, manage customer data, and monitor customer interactions across all channels—enabling precise targeting and personalized engagement.

## What You Can Do

### Customer List

Browse all your customers in one place. Search and filter customers by name, phone number, email, or custom attributes. View key information like status, tier, and preferred communication channel, with quick actions for managing individual customer records. See [Customer List](/documentation/customer-360/customers-list) for details.

### Create Customer

Add new customers to the system through three methods: single manual entry, bulk CSV import, or integration with external data sources. Build your customer database to enable targeted campaigns and communications. See [Create Customer](/documentation/customer-360/create-customer) for details.

### View Customer Details

View comprehensive customer profiles including contact information, demographic data, preferences, communication history, segment memberships, and engagement metrics. Update customer information and manage communication preferences from the detail view. See [View Customer Details](/documentation/customer-360/view-customer-details) for details.

### Customer Identity

Understand and manage customer segmentation fields. Learn what data is available for targeting, how fields are mapped, and how to use customer attributes to build segments and create targeted campaigns. See [Customer Identity](/documentation/customer-360/customer-identity) for details.

## Quick Start

**New to Customer 360?**

1. Go to [Customer List](/documentation/customer-360/customers-list) to see existing customers
2. Search for a customer by name or phone number
3. Click on a customer to [View Details](/documentation/customer-360/view-customer-details)
4. Ready to add customers? Follow [Create Customer](/documentation/customer-360/create-customer) for step-by-step instructions
