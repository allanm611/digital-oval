# Notification Settings

## Overview

The Notification Settings page allows you to configure your notification preferences, manage notification subscriptions, set Do Not Disturb (DND) rules, and choose your preferred communication channels. Control exactly what notifications you receive and how you want to be notified.

## Accessing Notification Settings

You can access Notification Settings in two ways:

1. **From the Notifications Page**
   - Go to [Notifications](/documentation/user-settings/notifications)
   - Click the **Notification Settings** button (top right)

## Notification Subscriptions

![Notification Subscriptions](/img/v1.2.4/notificationsetingssubscriptionscard.png)

### What Are Notification Subscriptions?

Notification subscriptions determine which system events trigger notifications for you. You can enable or disable notifications for different types of activities across campaigns, offers, segments, products, jobs, users, and system events.

### Managing Subscriptions

**Enable/Disable Notifications:**
- Each notification type has a toggle switch
- Check the box to **enable** notifications for that type
- Uncheck the box to **disable** notifications for that type
- Changes are saved when you click the **Save** button

**Notification Types Include:**
- Campaign notifications (creation, status changes, executions)
- Offer notifications (updates, activations)
- Segment notifications (creation, recalculation)
- Product notifications (updates)
- Job notifications (scheduling, execution results)
- User notifications (account activities)
- System notifications (platform updates)

## Communication Channels

![Notification Channels and Sound](/img/v1.2.4/notificationsetitngssound&channelcards.png)

### Preferred Notification Channels

Control which channels you prefer to receive notifications through:

**Available Channels:**
- SMS - Text message notifications
- Email - Email notifications with digest frequency options
- Desktop Notifications - In-app notification alerts

**SMS Alerts:**
1. Check **SMS Alerts** to enable SMS notifications
2. Uncheck to disable SMS notifications

**Email Notifications:**
1. Check **Email Notifications** to enable email notifications
2. Select your **Email Digest Frequency**:
   - **Instant** - Receive email notifications immediately
   - **Daily** - Receive a daily digest of notifications
   - **Weekly** - Receive a weekly digest of notifications
   - **Never** - Disable email notifications
3. Your preference is applied when you click the **Save** button

**Desktop Notifications:**
1. Check **Desktop Notifications** to enable in-app alerts
2. Uncheck to disable desktop notifications

### Notification Sound

Configure the notification sound for in-app alerts:

1. Check **Enable In-App Sound** to activate notification sounds
2. Select from available sounds: Default, Chime, Ding, Pop, Tone
3. Your selection is applied when you click the **Save** button

## Do Not Disturb (DND) Settings

![Message Delivery Settings](/img/v1.2.4/notificationsettingsmessagedeliverycard.png)

### What Is DND?

Do Not Disturb (DND) allows you to set quiet hours when you don't want to receive notification alerts. This is useful for evenings, weekends, or specific days.

### Configuring DND

**Enable/Disable DND:**
- Toggle the DND switch to turn it on or off
- When enabled, notifications are suppressed during DND hours

**DND Schedule Options:**

**Weekdays Only (Mon-Fri)**
- Notifications silent Monday through Friday
- Normal notifications on weekends

**Weekends Only (Sat-Sun)**
- Notifications silent Saturday and Sunday
- Normal notifications on weekdays

**Daily**
- Notifications silent every day during specified hours
- Same quiet hours apply to all days

**Custom Days**
- Choose specific days of the week for DND
- Set different hours if needed
- Useful for customized schedules

### Setting DND Hours

1. Choose your DND schedule type (Weekdays, Weekends, Daily, or Custom)
2. Set **Start Time** - When quiet hours begin (e.g., 21:00)
3. Set **End Time** - When quiet hours end (e.g., 08:00)
4. If using Custom Days, check the specific days you want DND active
5. Click **Save** to apply your settings

**Example:**
- Schedule: Daily
- Start Time: 21:00 (9 PM)
- End Time: 08:00 (8 AM)
- Result: No notifications between 9 PM and 8 AM

## Saving Your Settings

When you make changes to your notification settings:

1. Click **Save** to apply all changes (subscriptions, channels, DND settings, and sound preferences)
2. You'll see a success confirmation message when settings are updated successfully
3. Click **Cancel** to discard unsaved changes and revert to your previous settings
