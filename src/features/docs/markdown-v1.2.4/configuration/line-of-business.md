# Line of Business Overview

Line of Business represents distinct business units or divisions within your organization. Each line of business groups campaigns and communications related to a specific business area.

## What is Line of Business?

Line of Business allows you to:
- **Organize Campaigns** - Group campaigns by business unit or division
- **Assign Ownership** - Assign campaigns to specific business areas
- **Structure Organization** - Reflect your company's business divisions

## Managing Line of Business

Navigate to **Configuration → Line of Business** to manage all business lines.

![Line of Business List](/img/v1.1/configuration/lineofbusinesslist.png)

### Create Line of Business

Click the **Create** button to add a new business line.

![Create Line of Business](/img/v1.1/configuration/createlineofbusiness.png)

**Required Fields:**
- **Business Line Name** - The name of the business unit (max 100 characters)

**Optional Fields:**
- **Description** - Additional details about the business line (max 500 characters)

Click **Save** to create the business line.

### Edit Line of Business

Click **Edit** on any business line to update:
- Business Line Name
- Description

Click **Save** to apply changes.

### Delete Line of Business

Click **Delete** to remove a business line. The business line will be permanently removed from the system.

## Using Line of Business in Campaigns

When creating a campaign:

1. Go to **Create Campaign** → **Step 1: Definition**
2. Select **Line of Business** from the dropdown
3. Search by name or description to find the business line
4. Select the business line that owns this campaign
5. Continue with campaign configuration

The assigned line of business helps:
- Clarify which business unit owns the campaign
- Organize campaigns by business area
- Assign campaign responsibility to specific divisions
